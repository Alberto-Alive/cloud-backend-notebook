# Cloud & Backend Engineering Interview Prep

This repository contains my structured preparation for **backend / cloud engineering interviews**.

It’s designed to be:

- **Practical** – small, focused projects instead of huge monoliths  
- **Showable** – something I can share with interviewers/recruiters as proof of how I learn  
- **Reusable** – generic enough for consulting, SaaS, and product roles

The prep is split into **core modules** (what I focus on in 4 days) and **stretch modules** (nice-to-have if I continue after the sprint).

---

## 🧭 Goals

- Refresh and solidify **backend fundamentals** (APIs, auth, testing)
- Demonstrate understanding of **databases & caching** (SQL, indexes, Redis)
- Show familiarity with **containers, CI/CD, and cloud-native thinking**
- Practice **system design** for realistic, small-scale systems
- Optionally: highlight **performance/ML/HPC** topics if relevant to a role
